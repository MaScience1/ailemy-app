## ⚠ FIVE QUESTION PAPERS ARE BLOCKED, AND THE CHECK IS WRONG ABOUT WHY

`code_in_pdf` blocked all five. It reports "code not in document". That is not
what happened in at least two of them, and the check cannot tell the difference
between the two cases:

| file | pages | text in first 3 pages | code anywhere |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9MA0_31_que_20190615.pdf | 20 | **0 chars** | — no text at all |
| 9MA0_32_que_20190615.pdf | 20 | **0 chars** | — no text at all |
| 8FM0_01_que_20190514.pdf | 32 | **0 chars** | — no text at all |
| 9FM0_3C_que_20190621.pdf | 28 | 5195 chars | **not present in 28 pages** |
| 8fm0-27-que-20180518.pdf | 20 | 772 chars | **not present in 20 pages** |

The last two are demonstrably the right papers. `9FM0_3C` page 1 contains only
the print barcode `*P62674A0128*`; page 2 is mechanics content — a smooth
horizontal floor between two parallel walls — which is what Further Mechanics 3C
is. `8fm0-27` page 3 is decision-mathematics content about simply connected
graphs, which is what 8FM0/27 is. Their covers are images, so the paper code is
not extractable text.

So `code_in_pdf` conflates two different findings:

  - the document is the WRONG one, and
  - the document's cover is an IMAGE.

Only the first justifies a block. R24 says the check is not to be widened, so
the five stay quarantined and this is the project owner's ruling to make, not
mine. The evidence that bears on it:

  - Provenance is independent of the PDF's text. Each URL came from the index
    filtered to that exact code, entry and exam-series facet, and each basename
    carries the code. That is a stronger identity claim than a text search.
  - Two of the five are confirmed by subject content.
  - Three have no text at all and cannot be confirmed or refuted this way.
  - An alternative check exists: the Pearson print reference (`P62674A`) is
    extractable on the barcode page even when the cover is an image.
