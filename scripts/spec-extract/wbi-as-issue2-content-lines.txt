[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] in biology – including water, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] nucleic acids, providing a basis for many areas of biology. This
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] leads to the relevance of diet to health and the cardiovascular
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] system in particular. The unit includes cell membrane transport
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] processes, such as diffusion and active transport, proteins,
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] enzymes and protein synthesis. This unit also includes an
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] understanding of the genetic code and how mutations can result in
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] disorders. Students will also consider techniques for genetic
[p=20 x=199.2 Verdana [10]] screening and the associated ethical and social issues.
